What Pulse records

The four numbers that explain an API, per endpoint.

No sampling guesswork, no aggregate-only dashboards. Every endpoint gets its own latency distribution, error rate and throughput, with incidents placed next to the deploys that shipped them.

Latency percentiles

p50, p95 and p99 per endpoint, so a slow tail on /v1/checkout never hides behind a healthy average.

Alerting

Thresholds per endpoint. Pages to the channel your team actually watches.

A checkout endpoint and a search endpoint should not share one latency budget. Pulse lets you set thresholds per endpoint and routes alerts to Slack, PagerDuty and email.
