What we build

Most projects are one of these three. Plenty are a mix, and that’s fine.

  • A new website

    For: no site yet, or one you’ve outgrown

    We plan the pages with you, design for phones and desktops at the same time, and build it from scratch around the one thing you most want visitors to do.

    Usually includes

    • A page plan you sign off on
    • Design and build
    • A contact or quote form
    • Launch on your own domain

  • A redesign

    For: a site that’s dated or hard to use

    We start with what’s there: which pages people use, what’s out of date, what you keep explaining on the phone. We keep what works and rebuild the rest.

    Usually includes

    • A review of the current site
    • Clearer page structure
    • Old links pointed to new pages
    • Layouts that work on phones

  • The piece that’s missing

    For: a site that’s mostly fine

    Sometimes a site only needs one more thing to do its job. Where we can, we add it to the site you already have instead of starting over.

    For example

    • Contact and quote forms
    • Service pages
    • Galleries of past work
    • Menus and price lists

How a project goes

Four steps, and each one ends with something you can see. You’ll always know where things stand, and nothing gets built that you haven’t agreed to.

Talk through your idea →
  1. 01

    We listen

    A conversation about your business, the people you want to reach, and what a good result looks like to you. Bring your current site, one you admire, or a sketch on the back of an envelope.

    You get A short written summary, so you can check we heard you right.

  2. 02

    We agree on the shape

    We list every page and feature, and what each one is for, before any design starts. If something isn’t pulling its weight, we’ll say so.

    You get A scope and an itemized quote to say yes or no to.

  3. 03

    We build it where you can see it

    Design and code happen together on a private link. Open it on your own phone, click around, and send notes as we go, instead of waiting for a big reveal.

    You get A preview link that updates as the site comes together.

  4. 04

    We launch and hand over

    We put the site live on your domain, check it on real phones and computers, and walk you through the everyday edits.

    You get Logins in your name and a short guide written for your site.

When it’s finished, it’s yours.

Some studios hold on to the domain or the hosting, so you have to go back to them for every change. We’d rather you came back because you wanted to.

  • In your name

    Domain, hosting and accounts are registered to you. We ask for access; we don’t keep the keys.

  • Explained in plain English

    A short guide for your site: how to change text, swap photos and update your hours.

  • Checked on real screens

    Tried on actual phones, tablets and laptops before launch, not just a narrowed browser window.

Good to know before we start

If your question isn’t here, add it to the form below and we’ll answer it properly.

What kind of websites does CodeCraft build?

Sites for small businesses, independent makers, service providers and community projects. Usually a handful of pages with a clear job to do, run by someone who needs to change the opening hours without calling a developer.

Can you redesign a website I already have?

Yes. We look at what’s working and what isn’t before changing anything, keep the parts that earn their place, and make sure your old links still lead somewhere useful so you don’t lose people who bookmarked you.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the pages, features and content, so we don’t publish a single number. After the first conversation you get a written quote broken down by page and feature. You can see what each part costs and drop anything you don’t need.

How long does it take?

Mostly that comes down to scope, and to how quickly text and photos come together. You’ll get a timeline along with the quote, and we’ll tell you early if anything is likely to move it.

Will my website work on mobile?

Yes. Phone layouts are designed alongside the desktop ones, not squeezed in at the end. Before launch we check the site on real phones and tablets, and you can try it on your own throughout the build.

Do I need my text and photos ready first?

No. It helps to have something to start from, but rough notes, an old brochure or your current site are enough. We’ll tell you early what’s missing so it doesn’t hold up launch.
