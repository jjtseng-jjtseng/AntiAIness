How it goes

Four steps, no surprises.

This is the whole process. There's no "discovery phase" with a capital D, nothing happens behind a curtain, and you'll always know what we're working on this week.

Book the first call ↗
01

A thirty-minute call Phone or video, your pick

You tell us about the business. We ask about your customers, not your favourite colours. If we're not the right fit, we'll say so and point you somewhere better.

02

A one-page plan and a fixed price Within a few days

Pages, features, what we'll need from you, and what it costs. If you say yes, that number doesn't move unless the plan does.

03

Build in the open A private link from week one

You watch the site take shape and tell us what's wrong as you see it. Feedback is a reply to an email, not a formal review round.

04

Launch, then a check-in Everything handed over

We put it live, give you every file and login, and show you how to make small edits yourself. A month later we check how it's doing and fix anything that needs it.

House rules

Things we'll always do.

  • Write the code ourselves.No page builders, no pile of plugins. Fewer moving parts means less to break and less to pay for later.
  • Hand over everything.Domain, hosting, source files — in your name, with the passwords. If you ever leave, you take it all with you.
  • Say it plainly.If something's a bad idea, we'll say so and explain why. If we don't know, we'll say that too.
  • Build for the phone first.Because that's where most of your customers will see it, often on a slow connection, often in a hurry.

Questions

Straight answers, before you ask.

These come up on nearly every first call. If yours isn't here, put it in the brief and we'll answer it there.

What does a website cost?

We give a fixed price after the first call, once we know roughly how many pages and which features you need. We'd rather do that than publish a "from" price that turns out not to apply to you. The quote is one page long and covers everything through launch — there's no hosting surprise or "optional" essentials afterwards.

How long does it take?

Most sites go from first call to launch in four to eight weeks. The thing that stretches it is content — photos and words — so we help with both instead of waiting for them to arrive.

Do I have to write the words myself?

No. Send us what you have, even if it's a voice memo on the way to a job. We draft the copy, you correct it. You know the business; we know what people skim on a phone.

What do you actually build with?

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, written by hand. For sites that change often we add a simple editor so you can update hours, prices or news yourself. We stay away from heavy frameworks and plugin stacks because they're what makes sites slow now and broken later.

Can you redesign the site I already have?

Yes. We start by looking at what's working — there's usually something — keep the web addresses that matter so your search results don't disappear, and move the content over ourselves.

Will it work on phones?

It's designed on a phone first and checked on a cheap one, not just the newest. Then we make it look good on a big screen. Every site is also checked with a keyboard and a screen reader before launch.

What happens after launch?

You get every file and login, a short walkthrough of how to make small edits, and a check-in a month later. After that, you can email us for changes whenever you need them — small edits are quick and quoted up front.
