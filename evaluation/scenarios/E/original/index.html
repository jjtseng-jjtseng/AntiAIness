A few good questions

Good to know
before we start.

Every project is a little different. If yours has a question we didn't answer, tell us about it below.

What kind of websites does CodeCraft build?

We build custom websites for small businesses, independent makers, service providers, and community projects. The right scope depends on what your site needs to do.

Can you redesign a website I already have?

Yes. We can look at what's working, what isn't, and how a refreshed structure and visual direction can better serve your visitors.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the pages, features, and content your project needs. We start by understanding your goals, then work out a clear scope together before building.

Will my website work on mobile?

That's part of the design from the beginning. We plan each layout to feel clear and usable on phones as well as larger screens.
