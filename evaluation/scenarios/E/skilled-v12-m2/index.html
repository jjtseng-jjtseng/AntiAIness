Good to know before we start

Every project is a little different. If yours raises a question we haven't answered here, put it in the brief below.

What kind of websites does CodeCraft build?

Custom websites for small businesses, independent makers, service providers and community projects. Each one is built around a clear job, like getting a quote request or a volunteer sign-up. The right scope depends on what your site needs to do.

Can you redesign a website I already have?

Yes. We look at what's working, what isn't, and what your visitors are trying to do, then suggest a clearer structure and visual direction. The parts that already work don't have to go.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the pages, features and content your project needs. We start by understanding your goals, then agree a clear scope together before anything is built.

If you're unsure what to pick for “Roughly how many pages?” on the brief: 1–3 pages is often a single page, or home, services and contact. 4–7 adds things like separate service pages, a gallery or an about page. 8 or more usually means many services, locations or products.

Will my website work on mobile?

Yes, and it's planned that way from the start. We design each layout at phone size first and make sure buttons, forms and menus are easy to use with one thumb.
