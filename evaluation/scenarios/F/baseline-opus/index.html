How it works

Three habits, built in.

Most notes are written in a hurry and needed in a hurry. Notely is shaped around the three moments that matter: getting a thought down, tying it to what you already know, and finding it later.

Catch the thought before it goes.

One shortcut opens a blank note on top of whatever you were doing. Type, press Esc, and it lands in your Inbox — sort it into a notebook later, or never.

  • No title or folder required to save
  • Everything unsorted waits in the Inbox
  • Markdown shortcuts work as you type

Link notes the way you think.

Type [[ and start a title. Notely suggests matching notes, and every link appears as a backlink on the other side — so an idea can find its way back to where it started.

  • Suggestions as you type, across notebooks
  • Backlinks listed at the foot of each note
  • Link to a note that doesn’t exist yet to create it

Search that keeps up with you.

Results update with every letter, across titles, text and tags. Matches are highlighted in context, so you can tell which note you meant without opening five of them.

  • Open search from anywhere with ⌘ K
  • Filter by notebook or tag
  • Jump straight to the matching line

Also in Notely

  • **Markdown shortcuts

    Type # for a heading or ** for bold. Formatting stays out of the way.

  • [x]Checklists

    Turn any line into a to-do and tick it off without leaving the keyboard.

  • #Tags and notebooks

    Use one, both or neither. Nothing forces a folder structure on you.

  • ↑Pinned notes

    Keep the notes you open every day at the top of the sidebar.

  • ◐Light and dark

    Follows your system theme, or pick the one you prefer.

  • .mdMarkdown export

    Take any note or notebook out as plain Markdown files.
