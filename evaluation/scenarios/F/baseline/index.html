How it works

Three habits, one app.

  1. Capture

    Open Notely and start typing. New notes land in your Inbox so you never have to decide where something goes before you've written it.

  2. Connect

    Add a link or a tag when a note relates to something else. Over time your notes form a map you can wander through.

  3. Come back

    Search, backlinks, and a daily view bring old notes forward when they're relevant again, not just when you remember to look.

Principles

What we won't do with your notes.

Your notes stay yours

Export everything as plain files whenever you like. No lock-in, no proprietary format.

No feeds, no streaks

Notely is a tool, not a habit machine. There's nothing to scroll and nothing to keep up with.

Fast on purpose

Opening a note should feel instant. We treat speed as a feature and measure it like one.

Text first

Formatting is there when you want it and invisible when you don't. Writing comes before decorating.
