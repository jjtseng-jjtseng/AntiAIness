# Starlight Courier

A small, self-contained browser arcade game. No install, server, or internet connection needed.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser and press **Begin flight**.

- Fly with **WASD** or the **arrow keys**.
- Tap **Space** for a quick boost (it recharges in a couple of seconds).
- On touch screens, drag across the starfield to steer.
- Collect 8 aqua relay sparks and avoid the orange drones before time runs out.

Your best score is saved in this browser on this device.
